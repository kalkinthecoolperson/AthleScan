What is AthleScan AI?

AthleScan AI is an AI-powered technique analysis tool that helps young athletes understand and refine their movement mechanics. Athletes upload a short video, choose their sport, and name the skill they want to improve. The system then extracts key frames and evaluates body position, balance, timing, and sequencing against established elite-level technique standards.

Each analysis produces a prioritized report: an overall form score, current strengths, and the highest-impact corrections, each marked on the relevant frame with a clear coaching cue and a practice drill. AthleScan AI is designed as an accessible complement to coaching, not a replacement. Its feedback is based on still frames, so major technique changes should be reviewed with a coach.

How It Works

1 · Choose your sport and focus

Search for your sport and tap it, then pick a suggestion or type what you want analyzed, like your follow-through or footwork. Leave it blank for an overall check.

2 · Add your clip

Upload or record a 5-20 second clip of one clean performance, then use the Start and End sliders to trim it to the key moment. Add any notes you want AthleScan to know.

3 · Analysis

AthleScan pulls key frames from your clip, identifies the specific action you are doing, and compares your body position, timing and sequencing to how pros perform that exact action.

4 · Get your fixes

You get a form score, your strengths, and your top fixes, each marked on the frame with a cue to try and a drill. Then build a 5-day practice plan.

AI feedback is an estimate from a few still frames, not a substitute for a coach. Stop if anything hurts, and check big technique changes with a coach or parent.
